# GodotAndroidScanQRPlugin
A simple and fast QR &amp; barcode scanning plugin for Godot Android projects.

This lightweight Godot Android plugin lets you easily add QR and barcode scanning to your games and apps. With two simple scanning modes: • **Live camera scan**: Directly scan QR codes and barcodes using the device camera. • **Local image scan**: Pick QR/barcode images from your phone’s gallery and decode them instantly. Once scanned, the result is displayed clearly in a text field. You can tap the “Go” button to open links, URLs, or any supported content directly from the scan result. The plugin is fully open for customization: you can easily receive and handle scan results in your own code, enabling custom workflows like authentication, data parsing, or in-game actions tailored to your project’s needs. Perfect for adding quick authentication, in-game rewards, external link support, or any custom QR-based interaction to your Godot Android projects.
